A substantial warehouse unit on the harbour, most recently used as a design studio and available with vacant possession from December.

The main floorplate is open, with exposed brick, timber trusses and rooflights that give good natural light throughout the day. A mezzanine at the rear adds roughly 900 square feet of additional space, currently fitted as meeting rooms.

The building is Grade II listed, so any external alterations will need consent, but the internal layout has been changed twice in the past fifteen years without difficulty.
