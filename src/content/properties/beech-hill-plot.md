A half-acre building plot on the edge of the village, with outline planning permission granted in March for a single detached dwelling of up to 2,200 square feet.

The plot is broadly level, bounded by mature hedging, and enjoys open views south towards the Mendips. Mains water, electricity and drainage are available at the boundary; the successful buyer will need to arrange connections.

Reserved matters must be submitted within three years of the outline consent. Full planning documentation is available on request.
