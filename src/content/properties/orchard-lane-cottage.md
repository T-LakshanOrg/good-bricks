A Grade II listed cottage in the middle of the village, offered with no onward chain and in need of some sympathetic updating.

Downstairs there is a sitting room with an inglenook and woodburner, a separate dining room, and a kitchen at the rear looking out over the garden. Three bedrooms upstairs share a family bathroom; the third is a small double best suited as a child's room or study.

The garden is long and narrow, with three established apple trees and a stone outbuilding currently used for storage. Listed status means any works will require consent, and we would encourage buyers to speak to the conservation officer early.
