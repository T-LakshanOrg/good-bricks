A handsome five-bedroom Victorian villa on one of Redland's quieter residential roads, retained in the same family since 1978 and now ready for its next chapter.

The ground floor keeps its original proportions: a double reception with working fireplaces at either end, and a kitchen-diner extended into the side return with doors onto the garden. Upstairs there are four double bedrooms across two floors, plus a smaller room currently used as a study.

The walled garden faces south and is laid mostly to lawn with mature planting along the boundaries. The cellar is dry, tanked, and has head height suitable for conversion subject to the usual consents.
