Sarah founded the residential side of the business after two decades with a national agency, and still handles every period property valuation herself. She knows which Victorian terraces have the original sash windows worth restoring, and which conversions quietly cut corners in the nineties.

Clients come back to her because she gives an honest asking price rather than a flattering one. She works mainly across Clifton, Redland and the villages north of the city, and is happy to be called on a Saturday.
